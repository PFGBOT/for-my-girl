To My Bangaram 💜💙

From the deepest corner of my heart • Long Distance, Never Heart Distance

Nuvvu ippudu upset ga unnav ani naaku telusu. Maybe ippudu neeku evaritho matladali anipinchakapovachu, konchem alone ga undi nee thoughts ni sort chesukovali anipinchachu. And I want you to know one thing first…

Neeku space kavali ante, nenu aa space istaanu. ❤️

Ninnu force cheyyanu. Repeated ga “em ayyindi?”, “enduku matladatledu?” ani pressure pettanu. Neeku konchem time kavali ante teesuko. Silent ga undali anipiste undu. Cry cheyyali anipiste cry cheyyi. Nee feelings ni process cheskodaniki neeku entha time kavalo antha teesuko.

But there is one thing I can never do…

Nuvvu suffer avtunte, ninnu completely alone ga vadileyadam. 🫂

Space ivvadam ante ninnu vadileyadam kaadu.

Nuvvu konchem dooranga undali anukunte, nenu aa distance ni respect chestha. Kaani naa care matram aa distance tho dooram avvadu.

Endukante…

I love you. A lot. More than I can properly explain with words. ❤️

Manam long distance lo unnam. Every day physically pakkana undalem. Nuvvu upset ayinappudu nee pakkana kurchuni “it's okay” ani cheppalenu. Nuvvu edustunte nee tears wipe cheyyalenu. Nuvvu tired ga unte hug ivvalenu.

And maybe that's the hardest part for me.

Ninnu miss avvadam naaku everyday feeling.

Konni rojulu normal ga untayi. Kaani konni moments lo ninnu chala ekkuvaga miss avthanu. Nuvvu pakkana unte entha bagundedi ani anipistundi. Nee voice vinadaniki, nee tho matladadaniki, nee little little things anni share cheskodaniki chala miss avthanu.

But distance unna kuda…

naa care ki distance ledu. ❤️

Nuvvu happy ga unnapudu nenu nee happiness lo part avvalanukuntanu.

Nuvvu sad ga unnapudu, nee sadness ni completely solve cheyyalekapoyina, nee pakkana undalani anukuntanu.

Nuvvu upset ga unnapudu nenu “enduku ila unnava?” ani judge cheyyanu.

Instead…

“Nenu em cheyyagalanu?” ani think chestha.

Ninnu hurt chese things naaku teliste, avi nerchukunta.

Nee heart ni hurt chese words enti, actions enti, situations enti… avi observe chestha, understand cheskuntanu.

And if something I do hurts you, nenu defend cheskodaniki mundu, ninnu ardham cheskodaniki try chestha.

Because loving you ante just “I love you” ani cheppadam maatrame kaadu.

Ninnu ela love cheyyalo kuda nerchukovadam. ❤️

Nenu perfect kaadu.

Actually, nenu chala overthink chestha.

Sometimes small things kuda naa mind lo chala pedda questions ga maaripothayi. Nuvvu reply late chesthe, silent ga unte, konchem different ga behave chesthe… naa brain unnecessary things imagine cheyyadam start chestundi.

But I am trying.

Naa overthinking valla ninnu pressure cheyyakunda undadaniki try chestunna.

Every feeling ni immediately nee meeda pettakunda, first nenu nannu nenu calm cheskovadaniki nerchukuntunna.

Because I don't want my insecurities to become your burden.

Nuvvu already upset ga unna time lo, naa overthinking tho ninnu inka heavy feel cheyyinchadam naaku assalu ishtam ledu.

I want to become someone who gives you peace, not pressure.

Nuvvu “naaku konchem space kavali” ante…

I won't take it as “nuvvu nannu vadulukuntunnav.”

I will understand it as…

“Nuvvu konchem breathe avvali.”

And I'll give you that breath.

But at the same time, nenu oka distance lo nunchi aina nee pakkane untaanu.

Nuvvu alone ga undali anukunte, okay.

But alone ga suffer avvali ani matram nenu anukonu. 🫂

Nuvvu matladakapoyina parledhu.

Oka message pampakapoyina parledhu.

Nuvvu “I'm fine” ani cheppakapoyina parledhu.

Just know that somewhere, someone is still thinking about you, caring about you, and hoping you're okay.

That someone is me. ❤️

Nuvvu strong ga undalsina avasaram ledu.

Naatho everything okay ani pretend cheyyalsina avasaram ledu.

Nuvvu tired ga undochu.

Nuvvu weak ga undochu.

Nuvvu edvochuu.

Nuvvu angry avvochu.

Nuvvu silent ga undochu.

Nuvvu confuse avvochu.

Naa mundu nee real self ga undochu.

Nenu nee perfect version ni matrame love cheyyanu.

Nee good days ni love chestha.

Nee bad days ni kuda understand cheyyadaniki try chestha.

Nee smile ni love chestha.

Nee silence ni kuda respect chestha.

Nee happiness ni celebrate chestha.

Nee pain ni ignore cheyyanu.

And maybe nenu prati sari perfect words cheppalekapovachu…

Maybe sometimes nenu em cheppalo kuda teliyakapovachu…

But one thing will always remain the same:

I will care.

Ninnu care cheyyadam naaku obligation la anipinchadu.

It's something I genuinely want to do.

Nuvvu naaku important kabatti.

Nuvvu na life lo special kabatti.

Nuvvu nuvve kabatti. ❤️

Manam long distance lo unna…

Nuvvu naaku dooram kaadu.

Nuvvu screen opposite side lo unna person kaadu.

Nuvvu daily naa thoughts lo unde person.

Konni times phone lo nee name kanipinchina kuda smile vastundi.

Nee message kosam wait chestha.

Nee voice vinagane mood change avvachu.

And yes…

I miss you. A lot. Every single day.

But missing you doesn't make me want to control you.

It makes me want to understand you better.

So if you need space…

Take it, love.

Take all the time you need.

I'll respect it.

But whenever you feel like coming back…

I'll be here.

No anger.

No “why did you leave me alone?”

No guilt.

Just…

“Are you feeling a little better now?” ❤️

And if you're not better yet…

That's okay too.

We don't have to fix everything in one night.

We can take it slowly.

One conversation at a time.

One day at a time.

One little step at a time.

Because I don't want to be the person who is only there when everything is beautiful.

I want to be there when life feels heavy too.

Nuvvu padipothe, ninnu judge cheyyakunda lift cheyyadaniki try chestha.

Nuvvu confuse ayithe, patience tho listen chestha.

Nuvvu hurt ayithe, aa pain ni understand cheyyadaniki try chestha.

Nenu mistake chesthe, accept chesi better avvadaniki try chestha.

Because for me…

Loving you means choosing to understand you, again and again. ❤️

So please, ee one thing matram gurthupettuko…

✨ 💜 💙 ✨

Oka thing matram gurthupettuko...

Nuvvu alone kaadu. 🫂

💜 Space kavali ante space istaanu.
💙 Silence kavali ante silence istaanu.
💜 Time kavali ante time istaanu.

Kaani love and care kavali ante...
avi ikkade unnayi. 💜💙

💜💙

Nuvvu naatho matladakapoyina, I still care.

Nuvvu naatho unna, I still care.

Nuvvu happy ga unna, I care.

Nuvvu broken ga feel ayina, I care even more. ❤️

And no matter how far we are…

nee pain ni completely carry cheyyalekapoyina, danini alone ga carry cheyyalsina situation lo ninnu vadalanu. 🫂

Nenu nee problems anni solve chestha ani promise cheyyanu.

Nenu every situation lo perfect ga behave chestha ani kuda promise cheyyanu.

But one promise istaanu…

Ninnu ardham cheskodaniki try cheyyadam aapanu.

Ninnu hurt chese things nerchukoni, avi repeat cheyyakunda better avvadaniki try chestha.

Naa overthinking ni control cheskodaniki try chestha.

Nee space ni respect chestha.

Nee boundaries ni respect chestha.

And most importantly…

Nuvvu loved, valued, and cared for ani feel ayyela cheyyadaniki naa best istaanu. ❤️

So right now…

Don't force yourself to smile.

Don't force yourself to talk.

Just breathe.

Rest.

Take your time.

And whenever you're ready…

come back to me.

Oka “hey” aina chaalu.

Oka ❤️ aina chaalu.

Words lekunte kuda parledhu.

Nenu ikkade unna. 🫂

Because even when I give you space…

I will never make you feel like you've been left alone.

I love you.

I miss you.

I care about you.

And I am still learning how to love you better every single day.

Not because you asked me to.

Because you are worth learning for. ❤️

Always yours, through every distance & every storm,

Nee Blue Boy 💙
